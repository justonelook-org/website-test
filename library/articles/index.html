Research paper

The Radical Act of Inward Looking

Paul Freedman, M.S.W., R.S.W.; Jonathan Goldberg, M.S.W., R.S.W.; and Jaak Reichmann, M.D., FRCP(C)

This paper examines the Act of Inward Looking as a behavioral intervention for psychological suffering. It considers the method through the lens of in vivo exposure therapy and contrasts it with mindfulness-based approaches.

Originally published in Undivided: The Online Journal of Nonduality and Psychology, Vol. 1, Issue 4.

Student thesis · Psychology

The Just One Look Method: The Act of Inward Looking and the Self-Directed Attention Exercise

Niklas Lindström · Linnaeus University · 2021

A qualitative study of eight participants' experiences with the Just One Look Method, focusing on the Act of Inward Looking, the Self-Directed Attention Exercise, and reported consequences for mental health and well-being.

Independent thesis, bachelor level, Department of Psychology, Linnaeus University. Permanent identifier: urn:nbn:se:lnu:diva-100974.
